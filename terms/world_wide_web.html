World Wide Web (WWW)

Category: Fundamental Web Concepts

Definition

The World Wide Web is a wide-area hypermedia information retrieval initiative aiming to give universal access to a large array of documents.

Visual Representation

Origin & History

WWW was created by Tim Berners-Lee while he was working at CERN. His goal was to find a better way for scientists to share data across different computers.

WWW vs. The Internet

A critical distinction is that these two are not the same:

  • The Internet: The "Network of Networks"—the physical infrastructure of cables and hardware.
  • The WWW: A service that runs on top of the Internet, specifically used for accessing HTML documents via browsers.

Examples

If the Internet is the tracks of a railway system, the World Wide Web is the train that carries the information to your screen. Common examples include:

  • Web Browsers: Chrome, Safari, and Firefox are the tools we use to access the WWW.
  • Hyperlinks: The unique "web" of connections that allow you to jump from one document to another.
